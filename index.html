    How to play 🐾

    • Drag to orbit, scroll / pinch to zoom, right-drag to pan.
    • Hover a cat and wiggle the mouse to pet it — it will purr and melt into a loaf.
    • Click a cat to meet them and see their personality meters.
    • Pick the 🐟 Treat or 🧶 Yarn tool, then click the rug to toss something.
    • Use 🎨 Cat Creator to sculpt a kitten and adopt it into the room.
    • Hit ⚡ Zoomies when you simply need chaos.
